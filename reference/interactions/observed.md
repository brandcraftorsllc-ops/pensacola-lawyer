# Observed interactions

- The header remains fixed while scrolling. Desktop shows a Practice Areas
  dropdown; mobile replaces the full navigation with an Open menu button.
- On mobile, opening the menu reveals links to all primary routes. A fixed
  bottom Call Now and Consultation bar is present.
- Homepage and military-divorce FAQ items are collapsed initially and reveal
  one answer when activated.
- Resources has four filter buttons (Military Divorce, Child Custody &
  Deployment, Criminal Charges, Domestic Violence & Injunctions) and
  expandable answers. Each selected category displays its matching questions.
- The blog has All, Military Divorce, Family Law and Criminal Defense category
  filters, plus a Search articles field. Searching “DUI” returned the single
  matching DUI article.
- Homepage testimonial controls are Previous testimonial, three numbered
  selection buttons and Next testimonial.
- Case evaluation is a three-step intake flow. It starts with a matter choice,
  advances to detail questions, validates name/phone/email, and exposes a
  Submit Evaluation button on the final step. No submission was sent during
  reference inspection.
- Contact has full name, phone, email and message inputs and a Send Message
  button. No submission was sent during reference inspection.
- Tel, mailto and Google Maps directions links were identified. The map target
  uses the address 4502 Twin Oaks Drive, Pensacola, FL 32506.

Exact FAQ copy and answers are stored in `faq.json`.
